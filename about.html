About all4dp

An independent publication covering 4D printing and smart materials.

What we cover

all4dp reports new developments in 4D printing, shape memory materials, responsive hydrogels, liquid crystal elastomers, printed metamaterials and their uses, from laboratories and companies anywhere in the world. We also publish guides that explain the basics in plain language.

Editorial standards

  • Sources first. Every news item names the paper or announcement it is based on and links to it.
  • Our own words. Articles are summaries written by us. We do not copy text or figures from papers or press releases. Illustrations are our own unless credited.
  • No overstatement. We report what was measured, say when numbers were not given, and note the stage of the work, for example laboratory tests or animal studies.
  • Claims are attributed. Statements such as "first" or "best" are reported as the authors' own.
  • Independent. We are not affiliated with any printer maker, materials supplier or other publication, and we do not accept payment for coverage.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix the article and add a dated note saying what changed.

Suggest a story

Researchers and companies are welcome to send new papers and announcements for consideration. Contact details will be added here when the site launches.
